Given information:
- Angie bought 3 lbs. of coffee.
- Each lb. of coffee will brew about 40 cups of coffee.
- Angie drinks 3 cups of coffee every day.

Step 1: Calculate the total number of cups of coffee that the 3 lbs. of coffee can brew.
Total cups of coffee = 3 lbs. × 40 cups/lb. = 120 cups

Step 2: Calculate how many days the 120 cups of coffee will last Angie.
Cups of coffee consumed per day = 3 cups
Days the coffee will last = Total cups of coffee / Cups of coffee consumed per day
Days the coffee will last = 120 cups / 3 cups per day = 40 days

Therefore, the 3 lbs. of coffee that Angie bought will last her for 40 days.

Final answer: The 3 lbs. of coffee will last Angie for 40 days.
