Given information:
- Bob runs at 6 miles per hour
- Jim runs at 9 miles per hour
- Bob has a 1 mile head-start

To find the time it takes for Jim to catch up to Bob, we need to find the relative speed between them and the distance Jim needs to cover.

Step 1: Calculate the relative speed between Bob and Jim.
Relative speed = Jim's speed - Bob's speed
Relative speed = 9 miles/hour - 6 miles/hour = 3 miles/hour

Step 2: Calculate the distance Jim needs to cover to catch up to Bob.
Distance Jim needs to cover = Bob's head-start = 1 mile

Step 3: Calculate the time it takes for Jim to catch up to Bob.
Time = Distance / Relative speed
Time = 1 mile / 3 miles/hour = 1/3 hour

Step 4: Convert the time from hours to minutes.
Time in minutes = Time in hours × 60 minutes/hour
Time in minutes = 1/3 hour
