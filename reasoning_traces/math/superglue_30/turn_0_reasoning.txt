Given information:
- The company has 120 employees.
- 40% of the employees are women.
- 60% of the employees are men.

Step 1: Find the number of women in the company.
40% of 120 employees = 0.40 × 120 = 48 women

Step 2: Find the number of men in the company.
60% of 120 employees = 0.60 × 120 = 72 men

Step 3: Calculate the difference between the number of women and the number of men.
Difference = Number of men - Number of women
Difference = 72 - 48 = 24
