Given information:
- John buys 1000 balloons.
- Each balloon holds 10 liters of air.
- John buys 500-liter tanks of gas.

To find the number of tanks John needs to buy, we need to calculate the total volume of air required to fill all the balloons, and then divide it by the volume of each tank.

Step 1: Calculate the total volume of air required to fill all the balloons.
Total volume of air required = Number of balloons × Volume of air per balloon
Total volume of air required = 1000 × 10 liters = 10,000 liters

Step 2: Calculate the number of tanks needed to fill all the balloons.
Number of tanks needed = Total volume of air required / Volume of each tank
Number of tanks needed = 10,000 liters / 500 liters per tank = 20 tanks

Therefore, John needs to buy 20 tanks of 500-liter gas to fill all the balloons.
