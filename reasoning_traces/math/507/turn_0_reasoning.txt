Okay, let's work through this step-by-step:

Given information:
- The price of the steaks is $15 per pound.
- James buys 20 pounds of steaks.
- The deal is "buy one get one free", which means for every 2 pounds of steaks purchased, James only pays for 1 pound.

Step 1: Calculate the total weight of steaks James purchased.
Total weight of steaks = 20 pounds

Step 2: Calculate the number of pounds James has to pay for.
Since the deal is "buy one get one free", for every 2 pounds of steaks, James only pays for 1 pound.
Number of pounds James has to pay for = 20 pounds / 2 = 10 pounds

Step 3: Calculate the total cost of the steaks.
Total cost = Number of pounds James has to pay for × Price per pound
Total cost = 10 pounds × $15 per pound = $150

Therefore, James paid $150 for the 20 pounds of steaks.

Final answer: James paid $150 for the steaks.
