Okay, let's work through this step-by-step:

Given information:
- The parking lot is 400 feet by 500 feet.
- Only 80% of the total area is usable for parking.
- It takes 10 square feet to park a car.

Step 1: Calculate the total area of the parking lot.
Total area = Length × Width
Total area = 400 feet × 500 feet = 200,000 square feet

Step 2: Calculate the usable area for parking.
Usable area = Total area × 0.80 (since only 80% is usable)
Usable area = 200,000 square feet × 0.80 = 160,000 square feet

Step 3: Calculate the number of cars that can be parked.
Number of cars = Usable area / Area per car
Number of cars = 160,000 square feet / 10 square feet per car
Number of cars = 16,000 cars

Therefore, the parking lot can accommodate 16,000 cars.
