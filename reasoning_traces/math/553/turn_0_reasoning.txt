Given information:
- Emma buys 2 containers of milk every school day for lunch.
- Emma does not go to school on the weekends.

To find the total number of containers of milk Emma buys in 3 weeks, we need to:
1. Determine the number of school days in 3 weeks.
2. Multiply the number of school days by the number of containers of milk bought per day.

Step 1: Determine the number of school days in 3 weeks.
There are 5 school days in a week (Monday through Friday).
In 3 weeks, there are 3 × 5 = 15 school days.

Step 2: Multiply the number of school days by the number of containers of milk bought per day.
Number of containers of milk bought per day = 2
Number of school days in 3 weeks = 15
Total number of containers of milk bought in 3 weeks = 2 × 15 = 30

Therefore, Emma buys 30 containers of milk in 3 weeks.
