Okay, let's break this down step-by-step:

Given information:
- Matt and his son eat 3 days a week and use 1 plate each.
- On the remaining 4 days, Matt, his son, and his parents eat and use 2 plates each.

Step 1: Calculate the number of plates used on the 3 days when it's just Matt and his son.
Plates used on those 3 days = 1 plate per person × 2 people = 6 plates

Step 2: Calculate the number of plates used on the 4 days when Matt, his son, and his parents eat.
Plates used on those 4 days = 2 plates per person × 3 people = 24 plates

Step 3: Calculate the total number of plates needed by adding the plates used on the two different scenarios.
Total plates needed = Plates used on 3 days + Plates used on 4 days
Total plates needed = 6 + 24 = 30 plates

Therefore, Matt needs to buy 30 plates for his home.
