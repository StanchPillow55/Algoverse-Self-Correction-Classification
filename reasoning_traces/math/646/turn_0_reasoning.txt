Given information:
- The bus has a capacity of 200 people.
- On the first trip from city A to city B, the bus carried 3/4 of its capacity.
- On the return trip from city B to city A, the bus carried 4/5 of its capacity.

Step 1: Calculate the number of people the bus carried on the first trip from city A to city B.
3/4 of 200 people = 3/4 × 200 = 150 people

Step 2: Calculate the number of people the bus carried on the return trip from city B to city A.
4/5 of 200 people = 4/5 × 200 = 160 people

Step 3: Calculate the total number of people the bus carried on the two trips.
Total people carried = People on first trip + People on return trip
Total people carried = 150 + 160 = 310 people

Therefore, the total number of people the bus carried on the two trips is 310 people.
