Given information:
- Tom uses 10 weight plates, each weighing 30 pounds.
- The exercise machine uses special technology to make the weights 20% heavier on the lowering portion.

Step 1: Calculate the total weight of the 10 weight plates.
Total weight = 10 plates × 30 pounds per plate = 300 pounds

Step 2: Calculate the 20% increase in weight on the lowering portion.
20% of 300 pounds = 0.20 × 300 = 60 pounds

Step 3: Calculate the total weight felt on the lowering portion.
Total weight on the lowering portion = 300 pounds + 60 pounds = 360 pounds

Therefore, the weights felt 360 pounds when being lowered on the exercise machine.
