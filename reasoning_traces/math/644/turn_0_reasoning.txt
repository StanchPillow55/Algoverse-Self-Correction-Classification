Given information:
- The city's bus system carries 1,200,000 people each day.
- We need to find how many people the bus system carries for 13 weeks.

To solve this problem, we need to find the total number of people the bus system carries in 13 weeks.

Step 1: Find the number of days in 13 weeks.
There are 7 days in a week, so:
13 weeks × 7 days/week = 91 days

Step 2: Calculate the total number of people the bus system carries in 13 weeks.
Total people carried in 13 weeks = Number of people carried per day × Number of days in 13 weeks
Total people carried in 13 weeks = 1,200,000 people/day × 91 days
Total people carried in 13 weeks = 109,200,000 people

Therefore, the city's bus system carries 109,200,000 people for 13 weeks.
