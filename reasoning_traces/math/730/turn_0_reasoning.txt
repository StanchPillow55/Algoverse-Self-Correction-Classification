Okay, let's work through this step-by-step:

Given information:
- Jack bought 3 books a month at $20 each.
- He sells them back at the end of the year for $500.

Step 1: Calculate the total amount Jack spent on books in a year.
Number of books bought per month = 3
Number of months in a year = 12
Total number of books bought in a year = 3 books/month × 12 months = 36 books
Cost per book = $20
Total amount spent on books in a year = 36 books × $20/book = $720

Step 2: Calculate the amount Jack received from selling the books back.
Amount received from selling the books back = $500

Step 3: Calculate the amount of money Jack lost.
Amount lost = Total amount spent on books - Amount received from selling the books back
Amount lost = $720 - $500 = $220

Therefore, Jack lost $220 on the books.
