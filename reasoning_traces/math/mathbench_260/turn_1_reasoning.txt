Given information:
- There are 260 students in a school.
- 35% of the students are boys.
- The remaining students are girls.

To solve this problem, we need to find the number of boys and girls in the school.

Step 1: Calculate the number of boys.
35% of 260 students = 0.35 × 260 = 91 boys

Step 2: Calculate the number of girls.
Total students - Number of boys = Number of girls
260 - 91 = 169 girls

Therefore, the school has 91 boys and 169 girls.

Final answer:
The school has 91 boys and 169 girls.
