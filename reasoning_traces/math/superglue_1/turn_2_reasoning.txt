Sample Reasoning Question 1:

Given information:
- There are 15 apples in a basket.
- 3 apples are rotten.
- The remaining apples are to be divided equally among 4 people.

To solve this problem, we need to find the number of good apples and then divide them equally among the 4 people.

Step 1: Find the number of good apples.
Total apples - Rotten apples = Good apples
15 - 3 = 12 good apples

Step 2: Divide the good apples equally among the 4 people.
Good apples ÷ Number of people = Apples per person
12 ÷ 4 = 3 apples per person

Therefore, each of the 4 people will receive 3 good apples.

Final answer: Each of the 4 people will receive 3 good apples.
