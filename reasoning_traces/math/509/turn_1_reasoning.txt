Okay, let's break this down step-by-step:

1. The electricity price in Coco's town is $0.10 per kW.
2. Coco's new oven has a consumption rate of 2.4 kWh (kilowatt-hours).
3. Coco used the oven for a total of 25 hours last month.

To calculate the total cost, we need to find the total energy consumption and then multiply it by the electricity price.

Energy Consumption Calculation:
* Oven consumption rate: 2.4 kWh
* Total hours used: 25 hours
* Total energy consumption = 2.4 kWh x 25 hours = 60 kWh

Cost Calculation:
* Electricity price: $0.10 per kW
* Total energy consumption: 60 kWh
* Total cost = 60 kWh x $0.10 per kWh = $6.00

Therefore, Coco will pay $6.00 for using his oven for a total of 25 hours last month.
