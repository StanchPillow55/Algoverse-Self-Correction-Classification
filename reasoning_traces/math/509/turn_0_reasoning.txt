Given information:
- Electricity price in Coco's town is $0.10 per kW
- Coco's new oven has a consumption rate of 2.4 kWh (kilowatt-hours)
- Coco used the oven for a total of 25 hours last month

To calculate the total cost, we need to find the total energy consumption of the oven and then multiply it by the electricity price.

Step 1: Calculate the total energy consumption of the oven.
Total energy consumption = Consumption rate × Time used
Total energy consumption = 2.4 kWh × 25 hours
Total energy consumption = 60 kWh

Step 2: Calculate the total cost of using the oven.
Total cost = Total energy consumption × Electricity price
Total cost = 60 kWh × $0.10 per kWh
Total cost = $6.00

Therefore, Coco will pay $6.00 for using his oven for a total of 25 hours last month.
