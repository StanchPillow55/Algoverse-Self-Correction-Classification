Okay, let's solve this step-by-step:

Given information:
- Ping pong balls sell for $0.10 each.
- Johnny buys 10,000 ping pong balls.
- He gets a 30% discount for buying in bulk.

Step 1: Calculate the original cost of 10,000 ping pong balls without the discount.
Original cost = Number of ping pong balls × Price per ping pong ball
Original cost = 10,000 × $0.10 = $1,000

Step 2: Calculate the discount amount.
Discount amount = Original cost × Discount percentage
Discount amount = $1,000 × 0.30 = $300

Step 3: Calculate the final amount Johnny pays after the discount.
Final amount = Original cost - Discount amount
Final amount = $1,000 - $300 = $700

Therefore, Johnny pays $700 for 10,000 ping pong balls after the 30% discount.
