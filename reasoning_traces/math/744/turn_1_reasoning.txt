Given information:
- Yolanda leaves home for work at 7:00 AM, riding her bike at 20 miles per hour.
- 15 minutes after she leaves, her husband realizes she forgot her lunch and jumps in the car to bring it to her.
- He drives at 40 miles per hour and follows the exact same route as Yolanda.

Step 1: Calculate the distance Yolanda has traveled after 15 minutes.
Distance traveled by Yolanda = Speed × Time
Distance traveled by Yolanda = 20 miles/hour × (15/60) hour = 5 miles

Step 2: Calculate the time it takes for the husband to catch up to Yolanda.
Relative speed of the husband = 40 miles/hour - 20 miles/hour = 20 miles/hour
Time taken by the husband to catch up = Distance traveled by Yolanda / Relative speed
Time taken by the husband to catch up = 5 miles / 20 miles/hour = 0.25
