Given information:
- Each tube of lip gloss holds enough for 3 people's makeup.
- Marcy is bringing 6 tubs of lip gloss, each containing 2 tubes.
- This will be the exact amount she needs for everyone's makeup.

Step 1: Calculate the total number of tubes of lip gloss Marcy is bringing.
Number of tubs × Number of tubes per tub = Total number of tubes
6 tubs × 2 tubes per tub = 12 tubes

Step 2: Calculate the total number of people Marcy can do makeup for with the 12 tubes of lip gloss.
Total number of tubes × Number of people per tube = Total number of people
12 tubes × 3 people per tube = 36 people

Therefore, Marcy is painting makeup for 36 people.

Final answer: Marcy is painting makeup for 36 people.
