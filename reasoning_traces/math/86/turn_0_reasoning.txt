Given information:
- The soda costs $0.25 per ounce.
- Peter brought $2 with him.
- Peter leaves with $0.50.

To find the number of ounces of soda Peter bought, we need to calculate the total amount he spent on the soda.

Step 1: Calculate the total amount Peter spent on the soda.
Total amount spent = Amount Peter brought - Amount Peter left with
Total amount spent = $2 - $0.50 = $1.50

Step 2: Calculate the number of ounces of soda Peter bought.
Number of ounces = Total amount spent / Cost per ounce
Number of ounces = $1.50 / $0.25 per ounce = 6 ounces

Therefore, Peter bought 6 ounces of soda.
