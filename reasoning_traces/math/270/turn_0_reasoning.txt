Given information:
- Each phone call lasts 1 hour (60 minutes)
- Each call is charged 5 cents per minute
- Ayla talks to 50 customers per week

Step 1: Calculate the cost per call.
Cost per call = 5 cents per minute × 60 minutes = $3 per call

Step 2: Calculate the total cost for 50 calls per week.
Total cost per week = $3 per call × 50 calls per week = $150 per week

Step 3: Calculate the total cost for the month.
Assuming there are 4 weeks in a month:
Total cost per month = $150 per week × 4 weeks = $600 per month

Therefore, the phone bill at the end of the month for Ayla's 50 customer calls per week is $600.
