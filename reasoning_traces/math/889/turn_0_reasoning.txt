Given information:
- The new iPhone costs $800.
- Carrie can trade in her Samsung Galaxy for $240.
- Carrie can make $80 per week babysitting.

Step 1: Calculate the remaining amount Carrie needs to pay for the iPhone after the trade-in.
Cost of iPhone - Trade-in value = Remaining amount needed
$800 - $240 = $560

Step 2: Calculate how many weeks Carrie needs to work to earn the remaining amount.
Remaining amount needed / Weekly earnings = Number of weeks
$560 / $80 per week = 7 weeks

Therefore, Carrie needs to work for 7 weeks before she can purchase the iPhone.

Final answer: Carrie needs to work for 7 weeks before she can purchase the iPhone.
