Sample math problem 30:
A store sells 3 different types of pens: blue, red, and black. The store sells 15 blue pens, 12 red pens, and 18 black pens per day. What is the total number of pens sold per day?

To solve this problem, we need to find the total number of pens sold per day by adding up the number of each type of pen sold.

Given information:
- The store sells 15 blue pens per day.
- The store sells 12 red pens per day.
- The store sells 18 black pens per day.

Step 1: Find the total number of pens sold per day.
Total pens sold per day = Blue pens + Red pens + Black pens
Total pens sold per day = 15 + 12 + 18
Total pens sold per day = 45

Therefore, the total number of pens sold per day is 45.
