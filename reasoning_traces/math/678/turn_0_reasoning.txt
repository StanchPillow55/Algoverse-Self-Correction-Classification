Given information:
- Belinda can throw a ball at a speed of 20 feet/second.
- The ball flies for 8 seconds before hitting the ground.
- Belinda's border collie can run 5 feet/second.

Step 1: Calculate the distance the ball travels.
Distance traveled by the ball = Ball speed × Time
Distance traveled by the ball = 20 feet/second × 8 seconds = 160 feet

Step 2: Calculate the distance the border collie needs to run to catch the ball.
Distance the border collie needs to run = Distance traveled by the ball

Step 3: Calculate the time it takes the border collie to catch up to the ball.
Time taken by the border collie = Distance the border collie needs to run / Border collie speed
Time taken by the border collie = 160 feet / 5 feet/second = 32 seconds

Therefore, it will take the border collie 32 seconds to catch up to the ball.
