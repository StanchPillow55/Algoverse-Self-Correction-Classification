- The rope being sold is 20 feet long, but you lose 25% when lashing them together.

Total distance = Number of stories × Height of each story
Total distance = 6 stories × 10 feet/story = 60 feet

Step 2: Calculate the usable length of each rope after the 25% loss.
Usable length of each rope = 20 feet - (20 feet × 0.25) = 15 feet

Step 3: Calculate the number of pieces of rope needed.
Number of pieces of rope needed = Total distance / Usable length of each rope
Number of pieces of rope needed = 60 feet / 15 feet = 4 pieces

Therefore, Tom will need to buy 4 pieces of rope to lower the rope down the 6 stories.
