Step 2: Calculate the cost of the 3 additional apples you want to buy.
Cost of 3 additional apples = 3 × $0.50 = $1.50

Step 3: Calculate the total cost of all the apples.
Total cost = Cost of 5 apples + Cost of 3 additional apples
