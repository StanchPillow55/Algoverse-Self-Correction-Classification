Given information:
- Lee charges $33 per lawn mowed.

Step 1: Calculate the total revenue from mowing the lawns.
Total revenue from mowing lawns = Number of lawns mowed × Price per lawn
Total revenue from mowing lawns = 16 × $33 = $528

Step 2: Calculate the total tips received.
Total tips received = Number of customers who gave tips × Tip amount per customer
Total tips received = 3 × $10 = $30

Step 3: Calculate the total amount Lee earned mowing lawns last week.
Total amount earned = Total revenue from mowing lawns + Total tips received
Total amount earned = $528 + $30 = $558

Therefore, Lee earned $558 mowing lawns last week.
