To solve this problem, we need to determine the number of rooms required to accommodate all 30 students.

Given information:
- The class has 30 students.
- Each hotel room has two queen-size beds and a pull-out couch.
- Each queen-size bed can fit two students, and the pull-out couch can fit one student.

Step 1: Calculate the maximum number of students that can be accommodated in each room.
Maximum students per room = (2 students per queen-size bed × 2 beds) + 1 student on the pull-out couch = 5 students per room

Step 2: Calculate the number of rooms required to accommodate all 30 students.
Number of rooms required = Total number of students / Maximum students per room
Number of rooms required = 30 students / 5 students per room = 6 rooms

Therefore, the principal needs to book 6 rooms to fit all 30 students in the class.

Final answer: The principal needs to book 6 rooms to accommodate all 30 students.
