180 = 12x

Number of boys = 5x = 5 × 15 = 75
Number of girls = 7x = 7 × 15 = 105

Step 2: Calculate the amount of money each boy receives.
Total money given to the boys = $3900
Number of boys = 75
Amount of money each boy receives = $3900 ÷ 75 = $52
