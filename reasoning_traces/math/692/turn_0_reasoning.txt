Given information:
- Edric's monthly salary is $576.
- He works 8 hours a day for 6 days a week.

To find Edric's hourly rate, we need to first calculate the number of hours he works in a month, and then divide his monthly salary by the total number of hours worked.

Step 1: Calculate the number of hours Edric works in a week.
Hours per day × Days per week = Hours per week
8 hours × 6 days = 48 hours per week

Step 2: Calculate the number of hours Edric works in a month.
Hours per week × Weeks per month = Hours per month
48 hours × 4 weeks = 192 hours per month

Step 3: Calculate Edric's hourly rate.
Monthly salary / Total hours worked per month = Hourly rate
$576 / 192 hours = $3 per hour

Therefore, Edric's hourly rate is $3 per hour.
