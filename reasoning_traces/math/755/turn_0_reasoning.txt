Given information:
- Tom swims for 2 hours at a speed of 2 miles per hour.
- He then runs for half the time (1 hour) at 4 times the speed.

Step 1: Calculate the distance Tom swam.
Distance = Speed × Time
Distance = 2 miles/hour × 2 hours = 4 miles

Step 2: Calculate the speed at which Tom ran.
Running speed = 4 × Swimming speed
Running speed = 4 × 2 miles/hour = 8 miles/hour

Step 3: Calculate the distance Tom ran.
Distance = Speed × Time
Distance = 8 miles/hour × 1 hour = 8 miles

Step 4: Calculate the total distance Tom covered.
Total distance = Distance swam + Distance ran
Total distance = 4 miles + 8 miles = 12 miles

Therefore, the total distance Tom covered is 12 miles.
