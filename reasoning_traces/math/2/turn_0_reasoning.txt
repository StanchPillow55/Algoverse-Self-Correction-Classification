Since Betty had only half of the money she needs, that means she had $50 (half of $100).

Step 2: Calculate how much money her grandparents gave her.
Her grandparents gave her twice as much as her parents, which is 2 × $15 = $30.

Step 3: Calculate the total amount of money Betty has now.
Total money = Initial amount + Money from parents + Money from grandparents
Total money = $50 + $15 + $30 = $95

Step 4: Calculate how much more money Betty needs to buy the wallet.
Money needed = Cost of wallet - Total money
Money needed = $100 - $95 = $5

Therefore, Betty needs $5 more to buy the wallet.
