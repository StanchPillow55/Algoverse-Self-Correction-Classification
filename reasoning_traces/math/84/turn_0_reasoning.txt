To solve this problem, we need to find the total number of available rooms in the hotel, excluding the last floor that is unavailable.

Given information:
- The hotel has 10 floors.
- Each floor has 10 identical rooms.
- The last floor is unavailable for guests.

Step 1: Calculate the total number of rooms in the hotel.
Total number of rooms = Number of floors × Number of rooms per floor
Total number of rooms = 10 × 10 = 100 rooms

Step 2: Calculate the number of rooms available for guests.
Number of available rooms = Total number of rooms - Number of rooms on the last floor
Number of available rooms = 100 - 10 = 90 rooms

Therefore, Hans could be checked in to any of the 90 available rooms in the hotel.

Final answer: Hans could be checked in to 90 different rooms in the hotel.
