Okay, let's break this down step-by-step:

Given information:
- The builder purchased 7 boxes of bolts, with 11 bolts in each box.
- The builder purchased 3 boxes of nuts, with 15 nuts in each box.
- The builder finished the project 6 days early and had 3 bolts and 6 nuts left over.

Step 1: Calculate the total number of bolts purchased.
Total bolts purchased = 7 boxes × 11 bolts per box = 77 bolts

Step 2: Calculate the total number of nuts purchased.
Total nuts purchased = 3 boxes × 15 nuts per box = 45 nuts

Step 3: Calculate the number of bolts and nuts used for the project.
Bolts used = Total bolts purchased - Bolts left over = 77 - 3 = 74 bolts
Nuts used = Total nuts purchased - Nuts left over = 45 - 6 = 39 nuts

Therefore, the builder used 74 bolts and 39 nuts for the project.
