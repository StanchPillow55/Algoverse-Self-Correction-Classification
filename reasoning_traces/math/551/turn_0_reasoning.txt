To solve this problem, we need to find Arlene's average hiking pace in miles per hour.

Given information:
- Arlene hiked 24 miles on Saturday.
- She carried a 60-pound backpack.
- She is exactly 5 feet tall.
- She spent 6 hours hiking.
- The temperature during the day was 60 degrees Fahrenheit.

Step 1: Calculate Arlene's average hiking pace.
Average hiking pace = Total distance hiked / Total time spent hiking
Average hiking pace = 24 miles / 6 hours
Average hiking pace = 4 miles per hour

Therefore, Arlene's average hiking pace was 4 miles per hour.
