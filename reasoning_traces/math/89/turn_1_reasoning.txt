Given information:
- Krystian borrows an average of 40 books every day.
- Every Friday, the number of borrowed books is about 40% higher than the daily average.
- The library is open from Monday to Friday.

Step 1: Calculate the number of books Krystian borrows on a regular day (Monday to Thursday).
Regular daily average = 40 books

Step 2: Calculate the number of books Krystian borrows on Friday.
Friday's books = Regular daily average + 40% of regular daily average
Friday's books = 40 + (0.4 × 40) = 40 + 16 = 56 books

Step 3: Calculate the total number of books Krystian borrows in a week (Monday to Friday).
Total books borrowed in a week = (4 × 40) + 56 = 160 + 56 = 216 books

Therefore, Krystian borrows a total of 216 books in a week.
