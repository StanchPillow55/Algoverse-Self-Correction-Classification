Okay, let's break this down step-by-step:

Given information:
- Mr. Caiden requires 300 feet of metal roofing for his house repairs.
- Each foot of roofing costs $8.
- The supplier brings in 250 feet of metal roofing for free.

Step 1: Calculate the total cost of the 300 feet of metal roofing.
Total cost = Total feet of roofing × Cost per foot
Total cost = 300 feet × $8 per foot = $2,400

Step 2: Calculate the amount of roofing that Mr. Caiden has to pay for.
Amount of roofing to pay for = Total required - Free roofing
Amount of roofing to pay for = 300 feet - 250 feet = 50 feet

Step 3: Calculate the cost of the remaining 50 feet of roofing that Mr. Caiden has to pay for.
Cost of remaining roofing = Amount of roofing to pay for × Cost per foot
Cost of remaining roofing = 50 feet × $8 per foot
