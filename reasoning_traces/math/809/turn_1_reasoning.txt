Given information:
- Clover goes for a 1.5-mile walk in the morning and another 1.5-mile walk in the evening, every day.

To find the total distance Clover walks in 30 days, we need to:
1. Calculate the total distance walked in one day.
2. Multiply the daily distance by the number of days.

Step 1: Calculate the total distance walked in one day.
Morning walk: 1.5 miles
Evening walk: 1.5 miles
Total distance walked in one day = 1.5 miles + 1.5 miles = 3 miles

Step 2: Multiply the daily distance by the number of days.
Total distance walked in 30 days = 3 miles per day × 30 days = 90 miles

Therefore, Clover walks a total of 90 miles in 30 days.
