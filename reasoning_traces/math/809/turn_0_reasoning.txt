Given information:
- Clover goes for a 1.5-mile walk in the morning and another 1.5-mile walk in the evening, every day.
- We need to find the total number of miles Clover walks in 30 days.

Step 1: Calculate the total distance Clover walks in one day.
Distance walked in the morning = 1.5 miles
Distance walked in the evening = 1.5 miles
Total distance walked in one day = 1.5 miles + 1.5 miles = 3 miles

Step 2: Calculate the total distance Clover walks in 30 days.
Total distance walked in 30 days = Total distance walked in one day × Number of days
Total distance walked in 30 days = 3 miles × 30 days = 90 miles

Therefore, Clover walks a total of 90 miles in 30 days.
