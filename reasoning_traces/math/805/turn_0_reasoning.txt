Number of hours the theater is open / Duration of each movie = Number of movies shown on each screen

Step 2: Calculate the total number of movies shown throughout the day.
Number of movies shown on each screen × Number of screens = Total number of movies shown
4 movies × 6 screens = 24 movies

Therefore, the total number of movies shown throughout the day is 24 movies.
