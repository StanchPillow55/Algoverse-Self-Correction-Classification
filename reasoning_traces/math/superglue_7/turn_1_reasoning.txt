Given information:
- There are 12 apples in a basket.
- 3 apples are rotten.
- The remaining apples are to be divided equally among 4 people.

To solve this problem, we need to find the number of good apples and then divide them equally among the 4 people.

Step 1: Find the number of good apples.
Total apples in the basket = 12
Rotten apples = 3
Good apples = Total apples - Rotten apples
Good apples = 12 - 3 = 9

Step 2: Divide the good apples equally among the 4 people.
Number of people = 4
Good apples per person = Good apples / Number of people
Good apples per person = 9 / 4 = 2.25

Since we cannot have a fractional number of apples, we need to round down to the nearest whole number.
