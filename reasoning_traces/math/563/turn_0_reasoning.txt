To solve this problem, we need to find out how many people the roller coaster can accommodate in one run, and then divide the total number of people waiting in line by that number to determine how many times the ride operator will have to run the roller coaster.

Given information:
- There are 84 people waiting in line to ride the roller coaster.
- The roller coaster has 7 cars.
- Each car seats 2 people.

Step 1: Calculate the total number of people the roller coaster can accommodate in one run.
Number of cars × Number of people per car = Total number of people per run
7 cars × 2 people per car = 14 people per run

Step 2: Divide the total number of people waiting in line by the number of people the roller coaster can accommodate in one run.
Total number of people waiting in line / Number of people per run = Number of times the ride operator will have to run the roller coaster
84 people / 14 people per run = 6 times

Therefore, the ride operator will have to run the roller coaster 6 times to give everyone in line a turn.
