Okay, let's solve this problem step-by-step.

Given information:
- Carl types at a speed of 50 words per minute.
- He types continuously for 4 hours per day.
- We need to find how many words he can type in 7 days.

Step 1: Calculate the number of words Carl can type in 1 hour.
Words typed per minute × Minutes in 1 hour = Words typed in 1 hour
50 words per minute × 60 minutes = 3,000 words per hour

Step 2: Calculate the number of words Carl can type in 4 hours per day.
Words typed in 1 hour × Hours per day = Words typed per day
3,000 words per hour × 4 hours = 12,000 words per day

Step 3: Calculate the number of words Carl can type in 7 days.
Words typed per day × Days = Total words typed
12,000 words per day × 7 days = 84,000 words

Therefore, Carl can type 84,000 words in 7 days.
