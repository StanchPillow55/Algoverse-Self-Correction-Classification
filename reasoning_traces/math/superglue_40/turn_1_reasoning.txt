A factory produces 3 types of widgets: A, B, and C. Each widget type has a different production cost and selling price. The factory wants to determine the optimal production mix to maximize profit.

Given information:
- Widget A has a production cost of $2 and a selling price of $5 per unit.
- Widget B has a production cost of $3 and a selling price of $6 per unit. 
- Widget C has a production cost of $4 and a selling price of $7 per unit.
- The factory has a total production capacity of 1,000 units per day.

Determine the optimal production mix that will maximize the factory's daily profit.

Step 1: Calculate the profit per unit for each widget type.
Profit per unit = Selling price - Production cost
Widget A profit per unit = $5 - $2 = $3
Widget B profit per unit = $6 - $3 = $3
