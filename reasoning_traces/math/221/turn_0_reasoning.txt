Given information:
- Mira jogs 5 miles per hour.
- Mira jogs for 2 hours every morning.
- We need to find how many miles Mira can jog for five days.

Step 1: Calculate the distance Mira jogs in one day.
Distance jogged per hour = 5 miles
Time jogged per day = 2 hours
Distance jogged per day = 5 miles/hour × 2 hours = 10 miles

Step 2: Calculate the total distance Mira can jog for five days.
Total distance jogged = Distance jogged per day × Number of days
Total distance jogged = 10 miles × 5 days = 50 miles

Therefore, Mira can jog a total of 50 miles for five days.
