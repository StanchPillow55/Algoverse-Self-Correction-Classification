Given information:
- Jeff committed to run for an hour (60 minutes) a day during weekdays.
- On Thursday, he cut short his run by 20 minutes.
- On Friday, he was able to jog 10 minutes more.

Monday, Tuesday, and Wednesday: 3 days × 60 minutes = 180 minutes

Step 2: Calculate the minutes Jeff ran on Thursday.
Thursday: 60 minutes - 20 minutes = 40 minutes

Step 3: Calculate the minutes Jeff ran on Friday.
Friday: 60 minutes + 10 minutes = 70 minutes

Step 4: Calculate the total minutes Jeff ran for the week.
Total minutes = Monday, Tuesday, and Wednesday + Thursday + Friday
Total minutes = 180 minutes + 40 minutes + 70 minutes = 290 minutes

Therefore, Jeff was able to run for a total of 290 minutes that week.
