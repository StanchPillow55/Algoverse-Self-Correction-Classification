Given information:
- There are 170 apples in a basket.
- 35 apples are red.
- The remaining apples are green.

Find the number of green apples in the basket.

Step 1: Determine the number of green apples.
Total apples in the basket = 170
Red apples = 35
Green apples = Total apples - Red apples
Green apples = 170 - 35 = 135

Therefore, the number of green apples in the basket is 135.

Final answer: The number of green apples in the basket is 135.
