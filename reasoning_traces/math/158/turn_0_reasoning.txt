- Jerome is taking a 150-mile bicycle trip.
- He wants to ride 12 miles for 12 days.

Step 1: Calculate the total distance Jerome will ride in the first 12 days.
Distance per day × Number of days = Total distance in the first 12 days
12 miles × 12 days = 144 miles

Step 2: Calculate the remaining distance Jerome needs to ride to complete the 150-mile trip.
Total distance of the trip - Total distance in the first 12 days = Remaining distance
150 miles - 144 miles = 6 miles

Step 3: Determine how long Jerome needs to ride on the 13th day to complete the 150-mile trip.
Remaining distance = 6 miles

Therefore, Jerome will need to ride 6 miles on the 13th day to complete his 150-mile bicycle trip.
