Okay, let's break this down step-by-step:

Given information:
- Connie's grandmother's older brother was born in 1932.
- Connie's grandmother's older sister was born in 1936.
- The gap between Connie's grandmother and her sister is twice the gap between the older brother and the older sister.

Step 1: Find the gap between the older brother and the older sister.
Older sister's birth year - Older brother's birth year = Gap between them
1936 - 1932 = 4 years

Step 2: Find the gap between Connie's grandmother and her sister.
Since the gap between Connie's grandmother and her sister is twice the gap between the older brother and the older sister, we can calculate:
2 × 4 years = 8 years

Step 3: Find the birth year of Connie's grandmother.
Older sister's birth year - Gap between grandmother and sister = Grandmother's birth year
1936 - 8 years = 1928

Therefore, Connie's grandmother was born in 1928.
