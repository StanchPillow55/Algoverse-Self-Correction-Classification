Okay, let's break this down step-by-step:

Given information:
- Each employee gets 10 sick days and 10 vacation days per year.
- Mark uses half his allotment of both types of days in a year.
- Each day covers an 8-hour long workday.

Step 1: Calculate the total number of days Mark has allotted per year.
Total days allotted = Sick days + Vacation days
Total days allotted = 10 + 10 = 20 days

Step 2: Calculate the number of days Mark used in a year.
Days used = Half of total days allotted
Days used = 20 / 2 = 10 days

Step 3: Calculate the number of days Mark has left.
Days left = Total days allotted - Days used
Days left = 20 - 10 = 10 days

Step 4: Calculate the total number of hours' worth of days Mark has left.
Hours left = Days left × 8 hours per day
Hours left = 10 days × 8 hours/day = 80
