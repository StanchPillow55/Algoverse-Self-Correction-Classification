Okay, let's break this down step-by-step:

Given information:
- There are 6 people going on the airplane trip.
- Each person has 5 bags of luggage.
- Each bag weighs the maximum weight allowed, which is 50 pounds.
- The airplane can hold a total luggage weight of 6000 pounds.

Step 1: Calculate the total weight of the luggage the 6 people are bringing.
Total weight of luggage = Number of people × Number of bags per person × Weight per bag
Total weight of luggage = 6 × 5 × 50 pounds = 1500 pounds

Step 2: Calculate the remaining weight the airplane can hold.
Remaining weight the airplane can hold = Total weight capacity - Total weight of luggage
Remaining weight the airplane can hold = 6000 pounds - 1500 pounds = 4500 pounds

Step 3: Calculate the number of additional bags at maximum weight the airplane can hold.
Number of additional bags = Remaining weight the airplane can hold / Weight per bag
Number of additional bags = 4500 pounds / 50
