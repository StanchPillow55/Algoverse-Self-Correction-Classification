Given information:
- There are 400 mangoes on the tree.
- 3/5 of the mangoes are ripe.
- Lindsay eats 60% of the ripe mangoes.

Step 1: Calculate the number of ripe mangoes.
3/5 of 400 mangoes = (3/5) × 400 = 240 ripe mangoes

Step 2: Calculate the number of ripe mangoes Lindsay eats.
Lindsay eats 60% of the ripe mangoes = 0.60 × 240 = 144 ripe mangoes

Step 3: Calculate the number of ripe mangoes remaining.
Number of ripe mangoes remaining = Total ripe mangoes - Mangoes eaten by Lindsay
Number of ripe mangoes remaining = 240 - 144 = 96 ripe mangoes

Therefore, the number of ripe mangoes remaining is 96.
