Given information:

- The customer rents the car for 5 days.

Step 1: Calculate the total cost of the rental.
Total cost = Daily rate × Number of days
Total cost = $50 × 5 days
Total cost = $250

Therefore, the total cost of the rental for 5 days is $250.
