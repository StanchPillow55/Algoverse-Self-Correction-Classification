Given information:
- The length of the rectangle is four times its width.
- The area of the rectangle is 100 m².

Let's define the variables:
- Let the width of the rectangle be 'w' meters.
- Then, the length of the rectangle is '4w' meters.

Step 1: Write the formula for the area of a rectangle.
Area = length × width
100 = 4w × w
100 = 4w²

Step 2: Solve for the width 'w'.
100 = 4w²
w² = 100 / 4
w = √(100 / 4)
w = 5 meters

Step 3: Calculate the length of the rectangle.
Length = 4 × width
Length = 4 × 5 = 20 meters

Therefore, the length of the rectangle is 20 meters.
