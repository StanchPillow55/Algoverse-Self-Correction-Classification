Okay, let's solve this step-by-step:

Given information:
- John's website gets 30,000 visits per month for a normal 30-day month.
- John gets $0.01 per visit.

To find how much John makes per day, we need to:
1. Calculate the total revenue per month
2. Divide the total revenue per month by the number of days in a month to get the revenue per day

Step 1: Calculate the total revenue per month
Total revenue per month = Number of visits per month × Revenue per visit
Total revenue per month = 30,000 × $0.01 = $300

Step 2: Calculate the revenue per day
Revenue per day = Total revenue per month / Number of days in a month
Revenue per day = $300 / 30 days = $10

Therefore, John makes $10 per day from his website.

Final answer: John makes $10 per day from his website.
