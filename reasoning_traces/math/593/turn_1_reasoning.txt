2. On the second day, half of the snowdrift melted. So if the original depth was x inches, then after the melting, the depth was x/2 inches.

4. On the fourth day, another 18 inches of snow fell. So the total depth at the end of the fourth day was x/2 + 6 + 18 = 34 inches.

5. We can set up an equation to solve for the original depth x:
x/2 + 6 + 18 = 34
x/2 = 34 - 6 - 18
x/2 = 10
x = 20

Therefore, the depth of the snowdrift at the end of the first day was 20 inches.
