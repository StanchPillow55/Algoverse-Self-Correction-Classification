Okay, let's break this down step-by-step:

Given information:
- The teacher teaches 5 periods a day.
- The teacher works 24 days a month.
- The teacher is paid $5 per period.
- The teacher has been working for 6 months.

Step 1: Calculate the number of periods the teacher teaches per month.
Number of periods per day × Number of days per month = Number of periods per month
5 periods per day × 24 days per month = 120 periods per month

Step 2: Calculate the teacher's monthly earnings.
Number of periods per month × Pay per period = Monthly earnings
120 periods per month × $5 per period = $600 per month

Step 3: Calculate the total earnings for 6 months.
Monthly earnings × Number of months = Total earnings
$600 per month × 6 months = $3,600 total

Therefore, the teacher has earned a total of $3,600 over the 6 months.
