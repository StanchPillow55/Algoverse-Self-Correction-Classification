Given information:
- Mr. Williams bought 10 gallons of juice.
- Each gallon has 10 cups.
- 5 cups of juice were left at the end of the party.

To find the number of cups of juice that were drunk, we need to:
1. Calculate the total number of cups of juice that were available.
2. Subtract the number of cups of juice that were left.

Step 1: Calculate the total number of cups of juice that were available.
Total cups of juice = Number of gallons × Cups per gallon
Total cups of juice = 10 gallons × 10 cups/gallon = 100 cups

Step 2: Subtract the number of cups of juice that were left.
Cups of juice drunk = Total cups of juice - Cups of juice left
Cups of juice drunk = 100 cups - 5 cups = 95 cups

Therefore, 95 cups of juice were drunk at the party.
