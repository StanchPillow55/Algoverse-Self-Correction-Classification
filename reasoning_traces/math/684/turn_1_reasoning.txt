Given information:
- Grace can read a 200-page book in 20 hours.
- We need to find how long it would take her to read a 250-page book at the same constant rate.

Step 1: Find the rate at which Grace reads pages per hour.
Rate of reading = Number of pages / Time taken
Rate of reading = 200 pages / 20 hours = 10 pages per hour

Step 2: Find the time it would take Grace to read the 250-page book.
Time taken = Number of pages / Rate of reading
Time taken = 250 pages / 10 pages per hour = 25 hours

Therefore, it would take Grace 25 hours to finish reading the 250-page book at the same constant rate.
