Total crackers in 5 boxes = 5 boxes × 112 crackers per box = 560 crackers

Step 3: Calculate the number of nights the 5 boxes of crackers will last.
Number of nights = Total crackers in 5 boxes / Crackers used per night
Number of nights = 560 crackers / 10 crackers per night
