Given information:
- Every 30 minutes, the machine produces 30 cans of soda.
- We need to find how many cans of soda the machine can produce in 8 hours.

Step 1: Convert 8 hours to minutes.
8 hours = 8 × 60 minutes = 480 minutes

Step 2: Find how many 30-minute intervals are in 480 minutes.
480 minutes ÷ 30 minutes per interval = 16 intervals

Step 3: Calculate the total number of cans produced in 16 intervals.
Cans produced per interval = 30 cans
Total cans produced = 16 intervals × 30 cans per interval = 480 cans

Therefore, the machine can produce 480 cans of soda in 8 hours.
