Step 2: Calculate the yearly income from subletting.
Yearly income from subletting = $1,200 per month × 12 months = $14,400

Step 3: Calculate the yearly rent John pays.
Yearly rent = $900 per month × 12 months = $10,800

Step 4: Calculate the yearly profit.
Yearly profit = Yearly income from subletting - Yearly rent

Therefore, the profit John makes in a year is $3,600.
