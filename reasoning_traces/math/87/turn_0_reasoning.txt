Given information:
- John's cow initially weighed 400 pounds.
- The cow increased its weight to 1.5 times its starting weight.
- John can sell the cow for $3 per pound.

Step 1: Calculate the cow's new weight after gaining weight.
Initial weight = 400 pounds
New weight = 1.5 × 400 pounds = 600 pounds

Step 2: Calculate the difference in weight between the new and initial weights.
Difference in weight = New weight - Initial weight
Difference in weight = 600 pounds - 400 pounds = 200 pounds

Step 3: Calculate the difference in value between the new and initial weights.
Value of initial weight = 400 pounds × $3 per pound = $1,200
Value of new weight = 600 pounds × $3 per pound = $1,800
Difference in value = Value of new weight - Value of initial weight
Difference in value = $1,800 - $1,200 = $600

Therefore, the cow is worth $600
