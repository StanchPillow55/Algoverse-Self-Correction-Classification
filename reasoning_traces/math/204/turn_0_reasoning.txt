Given information:
- The stack of books is 12 inches thick.
- 80 pages is one inch thick.
- Jack has 6 books.

Step 1: Calculate the total number of pages in the stack of books.
Total thickness of the stack = 12 inches
Pages per inch = 80 pages
Total pages in the stack = 12 inches × 80 pages/inch = 960 pages

Step 2: Calculate the average number of pages per book.
Number of books = 6
Average pages per book = Total pages / Number of books
Average pages per book = 960 pages / 6 books = 160 pages

Therefore, the average number of pages per book is 160 pages.
