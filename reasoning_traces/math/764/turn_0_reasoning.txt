Given information:
- The dietitian ate three-fourths of her lunch during her lunch break.
- The total amount of food she had prepared for lunch had 40 calories.
- The recommended calorie intake by the FDA is 25 calories.

Step 1: Calculate the number of calories the dietitian ate.
Since the dietitian ate three-fourths of her lunch, we can calculate the number of calories she ate as follows:
Three-fourths of 40 calories = 3/4 × 40 = 30 calories

Step 2: Calculate the difference between the calories the dietitian ate and the recommended calorie intake.
Calories the dietitian ate - Recommended calorie intake = Difference in calories
30 calories - 25 calories = 5 calories

Therefore, the dietitian ate 5 more calories than the recommended amount.
