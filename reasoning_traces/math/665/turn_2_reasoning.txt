1. Bill picked 50 apples from the orchard with his wife and two children.

2. He sends each of his two kids to school with 3 apples for their two favorite teachers.
   * 2 kids x 3 apples each = 6 apples

3. His wife Jill bakes two apple pies, using 10 apples per pie.
   * 2 pies x 10 apples per pie = 20 apples

4. To find the number of apples Bill has left, we need to subtract the apples used for the teachers and the pies from the total apples picked.
   * Total apples picked: 50
   * Apples used for teachers: 6
   * Apples used for pies: 20

Therefore, Bill has 24 apples left.
