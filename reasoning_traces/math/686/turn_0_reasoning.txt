- The cottage costs $5 per hour to rent.

Step 1: Calculate the total cost of renting the cottage.
Total cost = Hourly rate × Number of hours
Total cost = $5 × 8 hours = $40

Step 2: Divide the total cost by 2 to find the amount each friend paid.
Amount paid by each friend = Total cost / 2
Amount paid by each friend = $40 / 2 = $20

Therefore, each friend (Jack and Jill) paid $20 for the rental of the cottage.
