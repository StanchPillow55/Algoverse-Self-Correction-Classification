Given information:
- The trader made a profit of $960 after a week of sales.
- To raise $610 for her next shipment, she splits the profit in half and solicits donations.
- She received a total donation of $310.

Step 1: Calculate the amount of profit the trader split in half.
Profit = $960
Half of the profit = $960 / 2 = $480

Step 2: Calculate the total amount of money the trader raised by adding the donation to the half of the profit.
Total amount raised = Half of the profit + Donation
Total amount raised = $480 + $310 = $790

Step 3: Calculate the amount of money the trader made above her goal.
Goal = $610
Amount made above the goal = Total amount raised - Goal
Amount made above the goal = $790 - $610 = $180

Therefore, the trader made $180 above her goal.
