Retail price = 1.8 × Wholesale price

Step 2: Substitute the given retail price of $36 into the equation.
$36 = 1.8 × Wholesale price
Wholesale price = $36 / 1.8
Wholesale price = $20

Therefore, the wholesale price of the pair of pants is $20.
