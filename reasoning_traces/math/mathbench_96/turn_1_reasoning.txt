Sample math problem 96:

Given information:
- There are 96 students in a classroom.
- The teacher wants to divide the students into groups of 8.
- The teacher wants to know how many groups can be formed.

To solve this problem, we need to find how many groups of 8 students can be formed from the total of 96 students.

Step 1: Divide the total number of students by the number of students per group.
Number of students / Number of students per group = Number of groups
96 students / 8 students per group = 12 groups

Therefore, the teacher can form 12 groups of 8 students each from the 96 students in the classroom.

Final answer: 12 groups
