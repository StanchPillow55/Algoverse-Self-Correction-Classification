Given information:
- The gummy bear factory manufactures 300 gummy bears per minute.
- Each packet of gummy bears has 50 gummy bears inside.
- We need to find how long it would take to manufacture enough gummy bears to fill 240 packets.

Step 1: Calculate the total number of gummy bears needed to fill 240 packets.
Total number of gummy bears needed = 240 packets × 50 gummy bears per packet = 12,000 gummy bears

Step 2: Calculate the time it would take to manufacture 12,000 gummy bears.
Time taken = Total number of gummy bears needed / Rate of production
Time taken = 12,000 gummy bears / 300 gummy bears per minute = 40 minutes

Therefore, it would take the gummy bear factory 40 minutes to manufacture enough gummy bears to fill 240 packets.
