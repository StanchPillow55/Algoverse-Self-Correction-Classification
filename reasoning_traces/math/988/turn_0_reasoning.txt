- The height of the box is 12 inches.
- The length of the box is 3 times its height.
- The length of the box is 4 times its width.

Step 1: Find the length of the box.
Height of the box = 12 inches
Length of the box = 3 × Height of the box
Length of the box = 3 × 12 = 36 inches

Step 2: Find the width of the box.
Length of the box = 4 × Width of the box
Width of the box = Length of the box / 4
Width of the box = 36 / 4 = 9 inches

Step 3: Calculate the volume of the box.
Volume = Length × Width × Height
Volume = 36 inches × 9 inches × 12 inches
Volume = 3,888 cubic inches

Therefore, the volume of the box is 3,888 cubic inches.
