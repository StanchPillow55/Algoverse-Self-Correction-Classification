Given information:
- The first rocket can travel 500 ft in the air.
- The second rocket can travel twice as high as the first rocket.

To find the combined height of the two rockets, we need to:
1. Calculate the height of the second rocket.
2. Add the heights of the two rockets together.

Step 1: Calculate the height of the second rocket.
Height of the second rocket = 2 × Height of the first rocket
Height of the second rocket = 2 × 500 ft = 1,000 ft

Step 2: Add the heights of the two rockets together.
Combined height of the two rockets = Height of the first rocket + Height of the second rocket
Combined height of the two rockets = 500 ft + 1,000 ft = 1,500 ft

Therefore, the combined height of the two rockets is 1,500 ft.
