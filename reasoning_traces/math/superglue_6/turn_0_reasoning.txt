Given information:
- The sum of two numbers is 15.
- The difference between the two numbers is 3.

Let's call the two numbers x and y.

Step 2: Write an equation for the difference between the two numbers.
x - y = 3

Step 3: Solve the system of equations to find the values of x and y.
From the second equation, we can isolate y:
y = x - 3

Substituting this into the first equation:
x + (x - 3) = 15
2x - 3 = 15
2x = 18
x = 9

Now we can substitute x = 9 into the second equation to find y:
y = x - 3
y = 9 - 3
y = 6

Therefore, the two numbers are 9 and 6.
