Given information:
- Harry has 50 books in his library.
- Flora has twice as many books as Harry.
- Gary has half the number of books that Harry has.

Step 1: Calculate the number of books that Flora has.
Flora's books = 2 × Harry's books
Flora's books = 2 × 50 = 100 books

Step 2: Calculate the number of books that Gary has.
Gary's books = 1/2 × Harry's books
Gary's books = 1/2 × 50 = 25 books

Step 3: Calculate the total number of books owned by the three of them.
Total books = Harry's books + Flora's books + Gary's books
Total books = 50 + 100 + 25 = 175 books

Therefore, the three of them own a total of 175 books together.
