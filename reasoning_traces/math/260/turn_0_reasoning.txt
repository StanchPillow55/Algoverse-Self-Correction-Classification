Time past noon = 6 hours
Increase in shadow length per hour = 5 feet
Total increase in shadow length = 5 feet × 6 hours = 30 feet

Step 2: Convert the shadow length from feet to inches.
1 foot = 12 inches
Shadow length in inches = 30 feet × 12 inches/foot = 360 inches

Therefore, the shadows from the building 6 hours past noon are 360 inches long.
