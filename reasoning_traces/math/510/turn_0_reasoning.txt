Given information:
- The pie shop charges $3 per slice of custard pie.
- Each whole pie is cut into 10 slices.
- The pie shop makes 6 whole custard pies.

To find the total money the pie shop will earn, we need to calculate the total number of slices and then multiply it by the price per slice.

Step 1: Calculate the total number of slices.
Number of slices per pie × Number of pies = Total number of slices
10 slices per pie × 6 pies = 60 slices

Step 2: Calculate the total money the pie shop will earn.
Total number of slices × Price per slice = Total money earned
60 slices × $3 per slice = $180

Therefore, the pie shop will earn $180 from selling the 6 whole custard pies.
