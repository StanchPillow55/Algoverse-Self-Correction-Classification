Okay, let's work through this step-by-step:

Given information:
- Clarissa needs to get 10 copies of a 400-page manuscript printed and bound.
- The printers charge $0.05 per page to copy it.
- The printers charge $5.00 per manuscript to have it bound.

Step 1: Calculate the cost to copy the manuscript 10 times.
Cost per page × Number of pages per manuscript × Number of copies = Total cost to copy
$0.05 × 400 pages × 10 copies = $200

Step 2: Calculate the cost to have the 10 manuscripts bound.
Cost per manuscript × Number of copies = Total cost to bind
$5.00 × 10 copies = $50

Step 3: Calculate the total cost to copy and bind the 10 manuscripts.
Total cost to copy + Total cost to bind = Total cost
$200 + $50 = $250

Therefore, the total cost to have the 400-page manuscript copied and bound 10 times is $250.
