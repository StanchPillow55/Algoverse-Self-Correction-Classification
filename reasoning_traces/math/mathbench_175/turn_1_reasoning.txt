Given information:
- There are 175 students in a school.
- The school wants to divide the students into 5 equal groups.
- How many students will be in each group?

To solve this problem, we need to divide the total number of students by the number of groups.

Step 1: Divide the total number of students by the number of groups.
Total number of students ÷ Number of groups = Number of students per group
175 ÷ 5 = 35

Therefore, each group will have 35 students.

Final answer: Each group will have 35 students.
