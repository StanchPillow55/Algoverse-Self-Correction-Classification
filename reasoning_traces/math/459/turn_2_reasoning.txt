Okay, let's break this down step-by-step:

Given information:
- A year ago, the total cost of buying a lawnmower was 2/5 times less than the cost it goes for now.
- The cost a year ago was $1800.
- Mr. Lucian wants to buy 4 such lawnmowers.

Step 1: Find the current cost of one lawnmower.
Let the current cost of one lawnmower be x.
A year ago, the cost was 2/5 times less than x, so the cost a year ago was 3/5 * x.
We know the cost a year ago was $1800, so:
3/5 * x = $1800
x = $1800 / (3/5)
x = $3000

Step 2: Calculate the total cost for 4 lawnmowers at the current price.
Total cost = 4 * $3000 = $12,000

Therefore, it would cost Mr. Lucian $12,000 to buy 4 such lawnmowers.
