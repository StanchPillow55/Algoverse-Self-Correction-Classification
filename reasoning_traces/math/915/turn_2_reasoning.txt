Okay, let's break this down step-by-step:

Given information:
- Each 60-watt bulb uses 60 watts of power per day.
- Allyn has 40 such bulbs in his house.
- The electricity rate is 20 cents per power watt used.

Step 1: Calculate the total power used by all the bulbs per day.
Power used per bulb per day = 60 watts
Total power used by all bulbs per day = 40 bulbs × 60 watts/bulb = 2,400 watts

Step 2: Calculate the total power used by all the bulbs in June (30 days).
Total power used in June = 2,400 watts/day × 30 days = 72,000 watts

Step 3: Calculate the total electricity cost for June.
Electricity cost per watt = 20 cents/watt
Total electricity cost in June = 72,000 watts × $0.20/watt = $14,400

Therefore, Allyn's total monthly expenses on electricity in June is $14,400.
