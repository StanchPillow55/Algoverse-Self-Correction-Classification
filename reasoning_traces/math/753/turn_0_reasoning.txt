Given information:
- The shop sold 29 bags of potatoes in the morning.
- The shop sold 17 bags of potatoes in the afternoon.
- Each bag of potatoes weighs 7 kg.

To find the total kilograms of potatoes sold for the whole day, we need to:
1. Calculate the total number of bags of potatoes sold.
2. Multiply the total number of bags by the weight of each bag.

Step 1: Calculate the total number of bags of potatoes sold.
Total bags sold = Bags sold in the morning + Bags sold in the afternoon
Total bags sold = 29 + 17 = 46 bags

Step 2: Multiply the total number of bags by the weight of each bag.
Total kilograms of potatoes sold = Total bags sold × Weight of each bag
Total kilograms of potatoes sold = 46 bags × 7 kg/bag = 322 kg

Therefore, the shop sold a total of 322 kilograms of potatoes for the whole day.
