Okay, let's break this down step-by-step:

Given information:
- John needs to take 2 pills a day.
- One pill costs $1.5.
- The insurance covers 40% of the cost.
- We need to find how much John pays in a 30-day month.

Step 1: Calculate the cost of 2 pills per day.
Cost of 2 pills per day = 2 × $1.5 = $3

Step 2: Calculate the total cost of pills for a 30-day month.
Total cost of pills for 30 days = $3 × 30 days = $90

Step 3: Calculate the amount covered by the insurance.
Amount covered by insurance = 40% of $90 = 0.4 × $90 = $36

Step 4: Calculate the amount John has to pay.
Amount John has to pay = Total cost of pills - Amount covered by insurance
Amount John has to pay = $90 - $36 = $54

Therefore, the amount John pays in a 30-day month is $54.
