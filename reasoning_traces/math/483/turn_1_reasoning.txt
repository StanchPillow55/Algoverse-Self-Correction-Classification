Okay, let's break this down step-by-step:

1. Andy started the year weighing 156 pounds.
2. He then grew 3 inches and gained 36 pounds.
   * So his new weight is 156 + 36 = 192 pounds.

3. Over the next 3 months, he lost an eighth of his weight every month.
   * In the first month, he lost 1/8 of 192 pounds = 192 * 1/8 = 24 pounds.
   * His new weight is 192 - 24 = 168 pounds.
   * In the second month, he lost another 1/8 of 168 pounds = 168 * 1/8 = 21 pounds. 
   * His new weight is 168 - 21 = 147 pounds.
   * In the third month, he lost another 1/8 of 147 pounds = 147 * 1/8 = 18.375 pounds (rounded to 18 pounds).
   * His final weight is 147
