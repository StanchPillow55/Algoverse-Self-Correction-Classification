Okay, let's break this down step-by-step:

Given information:
- Carrie works for $8 an hour
- Carrie works 35 hours a week
- Carrie has been working for 1 month
- Carrie wants to buy a bike that costs $400

Step 1: Calculate Carrie's weekly earnings.
Hourly rate × Hours worked per week = Weekly earnings
$8 × 35 hours = $280 per week

Step 2: Calculate Carrie's total earnings for 1 month.
Weekly earnings × Number of weeks in a month = Total earnings for 1 month
$280 × 4 weeks = $1,120

Step 3: Calculate the amount of money Carrie will have left over after buying the bike.
Total earnings for 1 month - Cost of the bike = Amount of money left over
$1,120 - $400 = $720

Therefore, Carrie will have $720 left over after buying the $400 bike.
