- John volunteers at a shelter twice a month.
- Each time he volunteers, he spends 3 hours at the shelter.

Step 1: Find the number of hours John volunteers per month.
Number of times per month × Number of hours per visit = Number of hours per month
2 times × 3 hours = 6 hours per month

Step 2: Find the number of hours John volunteers per year.
Number of hours per month × Number of months in a year = Number of hours per year
6 hours per month × 12 months = 72 hours per year

Therefore, John volunteers a total of 72 hours per year at the shelter.
