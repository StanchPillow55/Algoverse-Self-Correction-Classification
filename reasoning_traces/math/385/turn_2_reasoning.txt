Okay, let's break this down step-by-step:

Given information:
- The entrance fee to the strawberry fields is $4 per person.
- The standard price of a pound of strawberries is $20.
- Sally, Jenny, and Moses paid a total of $128 for their harvest.

Step 1: Calculate the total amount they paid for the strawberries before the entrance fee was deducted.
Total amount paid = $128
Entrance fee = 3 people × $4 = $12
Amount paid for strawberries = Total amount paid + Entrance fee = $128 + $12 = $140

Step 2: Calculate the total cost of the strawberries per pound.
Cost per pound of strawberries = $20

Step 3: Calculate the total number of pounds of strawberries they picked.
Total pounds of strawberries = Amount paid for strawberries / Cost per pound
Total pounds of strawberries = $140 / $20 = 7 pounds

Therefore, Sally, Jenny, and Moses picked a total of 7 pounds of strawberries.
