Okay, let's break this down step-by-step:

Given information:
- For each inch of rain, James collects 15 gallons of water.
- On Monday, it rained 4 inches.
- On Tuesday, it rained 3 inches.
- James can sell the water for $1.2 per gallon.

Step 1: Calculate the total amount of water collected.
Water collected on Monday = 4 inches × 15 gallons/inch = 60 gallons
Water collected on Tuesday = 3 inches × 15 gallons/inch = 45 gallons
Total water collected = 60 gallons + 45 gallons = 105 gallons

Step 2: Calculate the total amount of money James made from selling the water.
Price per gallon = $1.2
Total money made = 105 gallons × $1.2/gallon = $126

Therefore, the total amount of money James made from selling all the water is $126.
