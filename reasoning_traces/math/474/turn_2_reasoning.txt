Given information:
- The club has 30 members.
- Two-fifths of the members ordered lemon juice.
- One-third of the remaining members ordered mango juice.
- The rest ordered orange juice.

Step 1: Calculate the number of members who ordered lemon juice.
Two-fifths of 30 members = 2/5 × 30 = 12 members ordered lemon juice.

Step 2: Calculate the number of members who did not order lemon juice.
Total members - Members who ordered lemon juice = 30 - 12 = 18 members.

Step 3: Calculate the number of members who ordered mango juice.
One-third of the remaining 18 members = 1/3 × 18 = 6 members ordered mango juice.

Step 4: Calculate the number of members who ordered orange juice.
Total members who did not order lemon or mango juice = 18 - 6 = 12 members ordered orange juice.

Therefore, 12 members ordered orange juice.
