Given information:

- There are 2 vampires initially.
- Each vampire turns 5 more people into vampires every night.
- We want to find the total number of vampires after two nights.

Step 1: Calculate the number of vampires after the first night.
Initially, there are 2 vampires.
Each vampire turns 5 more people into vampires.
So, after the first night, there are:
2 vampires + (2 vampires × 5 people) = 12 vampires

Step 2: Calculate the number of vampires after the second night.
After the first night, there are 12 vampires.
Each vampire turns 5 more people into vampires.
So, after the second night, there are:
12 vampires + (12 vampires × 5 people) = 72 vampires

Therefore, after two nights, there are 72 vampires in the village of Willowton.
