Sample Reasoning Question 5:

Given information:
- There are 12 apples in a basket.
- 3 apples are red.
- The remaining apples are green.

Question: What percentage of the apples in the basket are green?

To solve this:
1) We know there are 12 apples total in the basket.
2) 3 of those apples are red.
3) The remaining apples must be green.

To calculate the number of green apples:
Total apples - Red apples = Green apples
12 - 3 = 9 green apples

Now to calculate the percentage of green apples:
Percentage of green apples = (Green apples / Total apples) x 100
= (9 / 12) x 100
= 0.75 x 100
= 75%

Therefore, the percentage of apples in the basket that are green is 75%.

Final answer: 75% of the apples in the basket are green.
