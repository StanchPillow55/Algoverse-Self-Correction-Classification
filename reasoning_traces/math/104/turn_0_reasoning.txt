Given information:
- There are 4 boat trips through the lake per day.
- The boat can take up to 12 people per trip.

To find the total number of people the boat can transport in 2 days, we need to:
1. Find the number of people the boat can transport in 1 day.
2. Multiply the number of people per day by 2 to get the total for 2 days.

Step 1: Find the number of people the boat can transport in 1 day.
Number of trips per day × Number of people per trip = Number of people per day
4 trips × 12 people per trip = 48 people per day

Step 2: Find the total number of people the boat can transport in 2 days.
Number of people per day × Number of days = Total number of people
48 people per day × 2 days = 96 people

Therefore, the boat can transport 96 people in 2 days.
