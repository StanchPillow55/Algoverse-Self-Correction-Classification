Step 1: Find the time it takes to wash 1 window.
Time to wash 4 windows = 10 minutes
Time to wash 1 window = 10 minutes / 4 windows = 2.5 minutes

Step 2: Find the total time it will take to wash all 64 windows.
Total time = Time to wash 1 window × Total number of windows
Total time = 2.5 minutes × 64 windows = 160 minutes

Therefore, it will take David 160 minutes to wash all 64 windows.
