Okay, let's work through this step-by-step:

Given information:
- Steve initially has a stock of 200 fish.
- He sells 50 fish.
- A third of the remaining fish become spoiled due to refrigerator issues.
- A new stock of 200 more fish arrives.

Step 1: Calculate the number of fish remaining after selling 50 fish.
Initial stock - Fish sold = Remaining fish
200 - 50 = 150 fish

Step 2: Calculate the number of fish that become spoiled.
One-third of the remaining fish become spoiled.
Remaining fish × 1/3 = Spoiled fish
150 × 1/3 = 50 fish

Step 3: Calculate the number of fish remaining after the spoilage.
Remaining fish - Spoiled fish = Fish remaining after spoilage
150 - 50 = 100 fish

Step 4: Calculate the total number of fish in stock after the new stock arrives.
Fish remaining after spoilage + New stock = Total fish in stock
100 + 200 = 300
