Okay, let's break this down step-by-step:

Given information:
- Sansa sells an 8-inch portrait for $5.
- Sansa sells a 16-inch portrait for twice the price of the 8-inch portrait.
- Sansa sells 3 portraits of 8-inch size and 5 portraits of 16-inch size per day.

Step 1: Calculate the price of the 16-inch portrait.
Price of 16-inch portrait = 2 × $5 = $10

Step 2: Calculate the total revenue per day.
Revenue from 8-inch portraits per day = 3 × $5 = $15
Revenue from 16-inch portraits per day = 5 × $10 = $50
Total revenue per day = $15 + $50 = $65

Step 3: Calculate the total revenue over 3 days.
Total revenue over 3 days = $65 × 3 = $195

Therefore, Sansa earns $195 every 3 days.
