Given information:
- Jonah bought 6 pineapples for $3 each.
- Each pineapple can be cut into 12 pineapple rings.
- Jonah sold 4 pineapple rings for $5 each.

Step 1: Calculate the total cost of the pineapples.
Total cost of pineapples = 6 pineapples × $3 per pineapple = $18

Step 2: Calculate the total number of pineapple rings.
Total number of pineapple rings = 6 pineapples × 12 rings per pineapple = 72 pineapple rings

Step 3: Calculate the total revenue from selling the pineapple rings.
Total revenue = 4 pineapple rings × $5 per ring = $20

Step 4: Calculate the profit.
Profit = Total revenue - Total cost
Profit = $20 - $18 = $2

Therefore, Jonah made a profit of $2
