- John buys 1/2 gallon jugs of cold brew coffee every 4 days.

Step 1: Convert 1/2 gallon to cups.

1/2 gallon = 2 cups

Step 2: Divide the total cups by the number of days.
Total cups in 4 days = 2 cups
Cups per day = 2 cups / 4 days = 0.5 cups per day

Therefore, John drinks 0.5 cups of coffee per day.
