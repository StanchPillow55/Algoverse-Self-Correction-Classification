Given information:
- Fatima's restaurant served 25 people food and drinks that week.
- 3/5 of the 25 people came to buy coffee.

Step 1: Calculate the number of people who came to buy coffee.
3/5 of 25 people = (3/5) × 25 = 15 people

Step 2: Calculate the number of people who did not buy coffee.
Total people served - People who bought coffee = People who did not buy coffee
25 people - 15 people = 10 people

Therefore, 10 people did not buy coffee in the restaurant.
