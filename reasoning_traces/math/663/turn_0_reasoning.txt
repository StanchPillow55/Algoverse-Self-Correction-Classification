- The flowerbed is rectangular.
- The width of the flowerbed is 4 meters.
- The length of the flowerbed is 1 meter less than twice its width.

Step 1: Find the length of the flowerbed.
Width = 4 meters
Length = 2 × Width - 1
Length = 2 × 4 - 1 = 7 meters

Step 2: Calculate the perimeter of the flowerbed.
Perimeter = 2 × (Length + Width)
Perimeter = 2 × (7 + 4) = 22 meters

Therefore, the amount of fence needed to enclose the flowerbed is 22 meters.
