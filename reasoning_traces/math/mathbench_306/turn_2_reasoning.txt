- There are 306 students in a school.

Step 1: Divide the total number of students by the number of classes.
Total number of students = 306
Number of classes = 17

Number of students per class = 306 ÷ 17
Number of students per class = 18

Therefore, there will be 18 students in each class.

Final answer: 18 students per class.
