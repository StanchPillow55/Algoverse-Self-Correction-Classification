Given information:
- Borris liquor store uses 90 kilograms of grapes every 6 months.
- Borris is thinking of increasing his production by 20%.

Step 1: Calculate the amount of grapes Borris uses in a year.
Amount of grapes used in 6 months = 90 kilograms
Amount of grapes used in a year = 90 kilograms × 2 = 180 kilograms

Step 2: Calculate the increase in production by 20%.
Increase in production = 20% of 180 kilograms
Increase in production = 0.20 × 180 kilograms = 36 kilograms

Step 3: Calculate the total amount of grapes Borris needs in a year after increasing his production.
Total amount of grapes needed in a year = Original amount of grapes used in a year + Increase in production
Total amount of grapes needed in a year = 180 kilograms + 36 kilograms = 216 kilograms
