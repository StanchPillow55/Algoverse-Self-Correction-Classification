   * Volume of the aquarium = length x width x height
   * Volume = 4 ft x 6 ft x 3 ft = 72 cubic feet

   * Halfway means the aquarium is half full, so the volume of water is 72 / 2 = 36 cubic feet.

3. The cat knocks over the aquarium, spilling half the water.
   * Half of 36 cubic feet is 18 cubic feet.
   * So the aquarium now contains 36 - 18 = 18 cubic feet of water.

4. Nancy comes back and triples the amount of water in the aquarium.
   * Tripling 18 cubic feet means the aquarium now contains 18 x 3 = 54 cubic feet of water.

Therefore, the final amount of water in the aquarium is 54 cubic feet.
