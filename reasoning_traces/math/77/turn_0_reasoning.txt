Given information:
- The boxer weighs 97 kg at 4 months from the fight.
- The boxer is on a diet that allows him to lose 3 kg per month until the day of the fight.

To find the boxer's weight on the day of the fight, we need to calculate how much weight he will lose over the 4 months.

Step 1: Calculate the weight loss per month.
Weight loss per month = 3 kg

Step 2: Calculate the total weight loss over 4 months.
Total weight loss = Weight loss per month × Number of months
Total weight loss = 3 kg × 4 months = 12 kg

Step 3: Calculate the boxer's weight on the day of the fight.
Weight on the day of the fight = Initial weight - Total weight loss
Weight on the day of the fight = 97 kg - 12 kg = 85 kg

Therefore, the boxer will weigh 85 kg on the day of the fight.
