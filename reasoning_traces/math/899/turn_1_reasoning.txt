Given information:
- Ben works 8-hour shifts
- It takes him 5 hours to build 1 rocking chair
- We need to find how many chairs he can build in 10 days

Step 1: Calculate the number of hours Ben can work in 10 days.
Number of hours per day × Number of days = Total hours
8 hours × 10 days = 80 hours

Step 2: Calculate the number of chairs Ben can build in 80 hours.
Total hours / Time to build 1 chair = Number of chairs
80 hours / 5 hours per chair = 16 chairs

Therefore, Ben can build 16 rocking chairs in 10 days.
