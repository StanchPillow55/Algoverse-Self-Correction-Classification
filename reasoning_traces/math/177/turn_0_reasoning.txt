Given information:
- James sold his $20,000 car for 80% of its value.
- James bought a $30,000 sticker price car for 90% of its value.

Step 1: Calculate the amount James received for selling his $20,000 car.
Amount received = 80% of $20,000
Amount received = 0.80 × $20,000 = $16,000

Step 2: Calculate the amount James paid for the $30,000 car.
Amount paid = 90% of $30,000
Amount paid = 0.90 × $30,000 = $27,000

Step 3: Calculate the total amount James was out of pocket.
Total amount out of pocket = Amount paid for new car - Amount received from selling old car
Total amount out of pocket = $27,000 - $16,000 = $11,000

Therefore, James was out of pocket $11,000.
