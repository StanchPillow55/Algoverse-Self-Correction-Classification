Given information:
- James buys 5 packs of beef
- Each pack is 4 pounds
- The price of beef is $5.50 per pound

Step 1: Calculate the total weight of the beef.
Total weight = Number of packs × Weight per pack
Total weight = 5 × 4 pounds = 20 pounds

Step 2: Calculate the total cost of the beef.
Total cost = Total weight × Price per pound
Total cost = 20 pounds × $5.50 per pound = $110

Therefore, James paid $110 for the 5 packs of beef.

Final answer: James paid $110 for the 5 packs of beef.
