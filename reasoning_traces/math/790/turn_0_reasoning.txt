Area of Zion's house = 1/2 × 40 cm × 20 cm = 400 cm²

Step 2: Calculate the total area of the three triangular houses.
Total area = Area of one house × Number of houses
Total area = 400 cm² × 3 = 1,200 cm²

Therefore, the total area of the triangular houses that Zion and his friends made is 1,200 cm².
