Given information:
- There are 6 chairs in each row in the church.
- There are 20 rows of chairs in the church.
- Each chair can hold 5 people.

To find the total number of people who can sit in the chairs when the church is full, we need to:
1. Find the total number of chairs in the church.
2. Multiply the total number of chairs by the number of people each chair can hold.

Step 1: Find the total number of chairs in the church.
Total number of chairs = Number of chairs per row × Number of rows
Total number of chairs = 6 × 20 = 120 chairs

Step 2: Multiply the total number of chairs by the number of people each chair can hold.
Total number of people = Total number of chairs × Number of people per chair
Total number of people = 120 × 5 = 600 people

Therefore, the number of people who can sit in the chairs for the church to be full is 600 people.
