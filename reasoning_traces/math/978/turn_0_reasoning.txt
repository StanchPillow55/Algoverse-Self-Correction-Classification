Given information:
- Stephen has 110 ants in his ant farm.
- Half of the ants are worker ants.
- 20% of the worker ants are male.

Step 1: Calculate the number of worker ants.
Total ants = 110
Half of the ants are worker ants
Number of worker ants = 110 / 2 = 55

Step 2: Calculate the number of male worker ants.
20% of the worker ants are male
Number of male worker ants = 55 × 0.20 = 11

Step 3: Calculate the number of female worker ants.
Total worker ants = 55
Male worker ants = 11
Female worker ants = Total worker ants - Male worker ants
Female worker ants = 55 - 11 = 44

Therefore, the number of female worker ants is 44.
