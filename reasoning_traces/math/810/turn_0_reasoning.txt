- Tyler initially has 21 CDs.

Step 2: Calculate how many CDs Tyler had left after giving away 7 CDs.
Initial CDs - CDs given away = CDs left
21 CDs - 7 CDs = 14 CDs

Step 3: Calculate how many CDs Tyler has after buying 8 new CDs.

14 CDs + 8 CDs = 22 CDs

Therefore, the final answer is that Tyler now has 22 CDs.
