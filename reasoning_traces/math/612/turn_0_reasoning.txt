- Ali has a total of 125 bottle caps.
- He has 50 red caps.

To find the percentage of green caps, we need to first find the number of green caps, and then calculate the percentage.

Total number of caps = 125
Number of red caps = 50
Number of green caps = Total number of caps - Number of red caps
Number of green caps = 125 - 50 = 75

Step 2: Calculate the percentage of green caps.
Percentage of green caps = (Number of green caps / Total number of caps) × 100
Percentage of green caps = (75 / 125) × 100 = 60%

Therefore, 60% of the caps are green.
