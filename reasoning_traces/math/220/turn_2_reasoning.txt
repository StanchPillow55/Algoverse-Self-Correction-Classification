Okay, let's solve this step-by-step:

1. We know that the car uses 20 gallons of gas to travel 400 miles.
2. This means the car's fuel efficiency is 20 gallons / 400 miles = 0.05 gallons per mile.
3. Mr. Montero's car has 8 gallons of gas in it.
4. The total distance he needs to travel is 600 miles, back and forth.
5. To calculate the total amount of gas needed, we multiply the fuel efficiency (0.05 gallons per mile) by the total distance (600 miles):
   * 0.05 gallons/mile × 600 miles = 30 gallons
6. Since Mr. Montero's car has 8 gallons of gas, the amount of additional gas he needs is:
   * 30 gallons - 8 gallons = 22 gallons

Therefore, Mr. Montero needs 22 more gallons of gas to travel 600 miles, back and forth.
