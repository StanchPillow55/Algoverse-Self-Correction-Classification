Sample Reasoning Question 48:
A factory produces 1,200 widgets per day. The factory operates 5 days per week. What is the total number of widgets produced in 3 weeks?

To solve this problem, we need to find the total number of widgets produced in 3 weeks.

Given information:
- The factory produces 1,200 widgets per day.
- The factory operates 5 days per week.

Step 1: Find the number of widgets produced per week.
Widgets produced per day × Days per week = Widgets produced per week
1,200 widgets × 5 days = 6,000 widgets per week

Step 2: Find the total number of widgets produced in 3 weeks.
Widgets produced per week × Number of weeks = Total widgets produced in 3 weeks
6,000 widgets × 3 weeks = 18,000 widgets

Therefore, the total number of widgets produced in 3 weeks is 18,000 widgets.
