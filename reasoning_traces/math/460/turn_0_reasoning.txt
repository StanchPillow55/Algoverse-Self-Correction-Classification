Given information:
- Last year, John made $100,000 and got a $10,000 bonus.
- This year, John makes $200,000.

To find John's total pay this year, we need to calculate his bonus for this year.

Step 1: Calculate the bonus percentage from last year.
Bonus percentage = Bonus / Salary
Bonus percentage = $10,000 / $100,000 = 0.10 or 10%

Step 2: Calculate John's bonus for this year.
Bonus for this year = Salary for this year × Bonus percentage
Bonus for this year = $200,000 × 0.10 = $20,000

Step 3: Calculate John's total pay for this year.
Total pay for this year = Salary for this year + Bonus for this year
Total pay for this year = $200,000 + $20,000 = $220,000

Therefore, John's total pay this year, including his salary and bonus, will be $220,000.
