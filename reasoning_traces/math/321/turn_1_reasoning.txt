Okay, let's work through this step-by-step:

Given information:
- Five months ago, Mike earned 10 times more money than Fred.
- Fred's salary five months ago was $1000.
- Mike's salary has increased by 40% since then.

Step 1: Calculate Mike's salary five months ago.
Since Mike earned 10 times more than Fred, we can calculate Mike's salary as:
Mike's salary = 10 × Fred's salary
Mike's salary = 10 × $1000 = $10,000

Step 2: Calculate Mike's new salary after the 40% increase.
Increase in Mike's salary = 40% of $10,000
Increase in Mike's salary = 0.40 × $10,000 = $4,000
New Mike's salary = Old Mike's salary + Increase in salary
New Mike's salary = $10,000 + $4,000 = $14,000

Therefore, Mike's salary now is $14,000.
