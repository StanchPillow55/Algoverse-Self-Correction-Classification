2) He plans to travel on two trips:
   - Trip 1: 30 km
   - Trip 2: 20 km

3) To calculate the total fuel needed for the two trips, we need to multiply the distance of each trip by the fuel usage per km.

For Trip 1 (30 km):
* Fuel needed = 5 liters/km × 30 km = 150 liters

For Trip 2 (20 km): 
* Fuel needed = 5 liters/km × 20 km = 100 liters

4) To get the total fuel needed, we add the fuel needed for each trip:
* Total fuel needed = 150 liters + 100 liters = 250 liters

Therefore, John should plan to use 250 liters of fuel for his two trips of 30 km and 20 km.
