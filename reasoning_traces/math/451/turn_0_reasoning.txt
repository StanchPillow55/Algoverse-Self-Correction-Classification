Given information:
- John buys a box of 40 light bulbs.
- He uses 16 of them.
- He then gives half of what is left to a friend.

Step 1: Calculate how many light bulbs are left after John uses 16.
Total light bulbs - Light bulbs used = Light bulbs left
40 - 16 = 24 light bulbs left

Step 2: Calculate how many light bulbs John gives to his friend.
Light bulbs left / 2 = Light bulbs given to friend
24 / 2 = 12 light bulbs given to friend

Step 3: Calculate how many light bulbs John has left.
Light bulbs left - Light bulbs given to friend = Light bulbs John has left
24 - 12 = 12 light bulbs left

Therefore, John has 12 light bulbs left.
