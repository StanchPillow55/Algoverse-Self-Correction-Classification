Given information:
- A vampire needs 7 gallons of blood per week to survive.
- The vampire sucks 2 pints of blood per person.

Step 1: Convert the required blood per week from gallons to pints.
1 gallon = 8 pints
7 gallons = 7 × 8 = 56 pints

Step 2: Calculate the number of people the vampire needs to suck per week.
Number of people per week = Total blood required per week / Blood per person
Number of people per week = 56 pints / 2 pints per person = 28 people

Step 3: Calculate the number of people the vampire needs to suck per day.
Number of people per day = Number of people per week / 7 days
Number of people per day = 28 people / 7 days = 4 people

Therefore, the vampire needs to suck 4 people per day to survive.
