Given information:
- Janet's previous movie was 2 hours long (120 minutes)
- The new movie is 60% longer than the previous movie
- The previous movie cost $50 per minute to film
- The new movie costs twice as much per minute to film as the previous movie

Step 1: Calculate the length of the new movie
Previous movie length = 120 minutes
Increase in length = 60% of 120 minutes = 0.6 × 120 = 72 minutes
New movie length = 120 minutes + 72 minutes = 192 minutes

Step 2: Calculate the cost per minute for the new movie
Cost per minute for previous movie = $50
Cost per minute for new movie = 2 × $50 = $100

Step 3: Calculate the total cost of the new movie
Total cost = Cost per minute × Total minutes
Total cost = $100 × 192 minutes = $19,200

Therefore, the total amount of money required to film Janet's entire newest film is $19,200.
