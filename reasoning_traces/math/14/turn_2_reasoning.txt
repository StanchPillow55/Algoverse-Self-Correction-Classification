Okay, let's solve this step-by-step:

Given information:
- Joy can read 8 pages in 20 minutes.
- We need to find how many hours it will take her to read 120 pages.

Step 1: Find the rate at which Joy reads pages per minute.
Rate of reading = 8 pages / 20 minutes = 0.4 pages per minute

Step 2: Find the total time it will take Joy to read 120 pages.
Total time = Total pages / Rate of reading
Total time = 120 pages / 0.4 pages per minute = 300 minutes

Step 3: Convert the total time from minutes to hours.
Total time in hours = 300 minutes / 60 minutes per hour = 5 hours

Therefore, it will take Joy 5 hours to read 120 pages.
