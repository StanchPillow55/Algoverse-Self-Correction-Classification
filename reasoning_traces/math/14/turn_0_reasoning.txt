- Joy can read 8 pages of a book in 20 minutes.
- We need to find how many hours it will take her to read 120 pages.

Step 1: Find the rate at which Joy reads pages.

Rate of reading = 8 pages / 20 minutes
Rate of reading = 0.4 pages per minute

Step 2: Find the time it will take Joy to read 120 pages.
Time taken = Number of pages / Rate of reading
Time taken = 120 pages / 0.4 pages per minute
Time taken = 300 minutes

Step 3: Convert the time from minutes to hours.
Time in hours = Time in minutes / 60
Time in hours = 300 minutes / 60
Time in hours = 5 hours

Therefore, it will take Joy 5 hours to read 120 pages.
