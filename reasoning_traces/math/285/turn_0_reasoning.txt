Given information:
- John wants to finish a show in 5 days.
- There are 20 episodes in the show.
- Each episode is 30 minutes long.

To find how many hours John has to watch per day, we need to:
1. Calculate the total duration of the show.
2. Divide the total duration by the number of days.

Step 1: Calculate the total duration of the show.
Total duration = Number of episodes × Duration of each episode
Total duration = 20 episodes × 30 minutes/episode
Total duration = 600 minutes

Step 2: Convert the total duration from minutes to hours.
Total duration in hours = Total duration in minutes / 60 minutes/hour
Total duration in hours = 600 minutes / 60 minutes/hour
Total duration in hours = 10 hours

Step 3: Divide the total duration by the number of days.
Hours per day = Total duration in hours / Number of days
Hours per day = 10 hours / 5 days
Hours per day = 2 hours
