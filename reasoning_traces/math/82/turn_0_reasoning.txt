- Irene earns $500 if she works for 40 hours a week.
- She gets an extra $20 for every hour of overtime.
- Irene worked 50 hours last week.

Step 1: Calculate the number of regular hours and overtime hours.
Regular hours = 40 hours
Overtime hours = 50 hours - 40 hours = 10 hours

Step 2: Calculate the base pay for the regular hours.
Base pay = $500 for 40 hours

Step 3: Calculate the overtime pay.
Overtime pay = $20 per hour × 10 hours = $200

Step 4: Calculate the total income.
Total income = Base pay + Overtime pay
Total income = $500 + $200 = $700

Therefore, Irene's total income for working 50 hours last week is $700.
