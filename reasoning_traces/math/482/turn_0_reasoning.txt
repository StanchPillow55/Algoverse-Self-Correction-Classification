Okay, let's break this down step-by-step:

Given information:
- Ian does 5 laps around the complex every night.
- Each lap is 100 feet.
- It takes 25 feet of jogging to burn 1 calorie.
- We need to find how many calories Ian burns after 5 days of jogging.

Step 1: Calculate the total distance Ian jogs in one night.
Distance per lap × Number of laps = Total distance per night
100 feet × 5 laps = 500 feet

Step 2: Calculate the number of calories burned in one night.
Total distance per night / Distance per calorie = Calories burned per night
500 feet / 25 feet per calorie = 20 calories

Step 3: Calculate the total calories burned over 5 days.
Calories burned per night × Number of days = Total calories burned
20 calories × 5 days = 100 calories

Therefore, after 5 days of jogging, Ian burns a total of 100 calories.
