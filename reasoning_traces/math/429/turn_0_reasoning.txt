Okay, let's work through this step-by-step:

Given information:
- There are 2300 different books in the school's library.
- 80% of all the books are in English.
- 60% of the English books were published in the country.

Step 1: Calculate the number of English-language books.
80% of 2300 books = 0.80 × 2300 = 1840 English-language books

Step 2: Calculate the number of English-language books published in the country.
60% of 1840 books = 0.60 × 1840 = 1104 English-language books published in the country

Step 3: Calculate the number of English-language books published outside the country.
Number of English-language books published outside the country = Total English-language books - English-language books published in the country
Number of English-language books published outside the country = 1840 - 1104 = 736 books

Therefore, the number of English-language books that have been published outside the country is 736.
