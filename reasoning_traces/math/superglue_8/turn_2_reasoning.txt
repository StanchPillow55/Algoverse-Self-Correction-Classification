Sample Reasoning Question 8:

Given information:
- There are 12 apples in a basket.
- 3 apples are red.
- The remaining apples are green.

Question: What percentage of the apples in the basket are green?

To solve this problem, we need to find the number of green apples and then calculate the percentage of green apples out of the total number of apples.

Step 1: Find the number of green apples.
Total apples in the basket = 12
Red apples = 3
Green apples = Total apples - Red apples
Green apples = 12 - 3 = 9

Step 2: Calculate the percentage of green apples.
Percentage of green apples = (Number of green apples / Total apples) × 100
Percentage of green apples = (9 / 12) × 100 = 75%

Therefore, 75% of the apples in the basket are green.
