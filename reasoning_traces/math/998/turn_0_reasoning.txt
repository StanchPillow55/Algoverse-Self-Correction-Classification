To solve this problem, we need to find the net rate at which the pool is filled and then calculate the time it takes to fill the pool.

Given information:
- The pool holds 60 gallons of water when full.
- The garden hose provides water at a rate of 1.6 gallons per minute.
- The pool has a small hole that leaks water at a rate of 0.1 gallons per minute.

Step 1: Find the net rate at which the pool is filled.
Net rate = Rate of water input - Rate of water leakage
Net rate = 1.6 gallons/minute - 0.1 gallons/minute
Net rate = 1.5 gallons/minute

Step 2: Calculate the time it takes to fill the pool.
Time to fill the pool = Volume of the pool / Net rate of filling
Time to fill the pool = 60 gallons / 1.5 gallons/minute
Time to fill the pool = 40 minutes

Therefore, it will take Lydia 40 minutes to fill the pool.
