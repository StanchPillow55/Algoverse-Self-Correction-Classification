Okay, let's work through this step-by-step:

Given information:
- Children 6-11 years old spend 45 minutes each day watching television.
- They are allowed to watch television 4 days a week.
- We need to find how many hours they watch television in 2 weeks.

Step 1: Convert the daily television watching time from minutes to hours.
45 minutes per day = 45 / 60 = 0.75 hours per day

Step 2: Calculate the total television watching time per week.
Television watching time per week = 0.75 hours per day × 4 days per week = 3 hours per week

Step 3: Calculate the total television watching time in 2 weeks.
Television watching time in 2 weeks = 3 hours per week × 2 weeks = 6 hours

Therefore, children 6-11 years old watch 6 hours of television in 2 weeks if they are allowed to watch television 4 days a week.
