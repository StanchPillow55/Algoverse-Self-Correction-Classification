Okay, let's break this down step-by-step:

Given information:
- Children 6-11 years old spend 45 minutes each day watching television.
- They are allowed to watch television 4 days a week.
- We need to find how many hours they watch television in 2 weeks.

Step 1: Calculate the total minutes of television watched per week.
Minutes per day × Days per week = Minutes per week
45 minutes × 4 days = 180 minutes per week

Step 2: Convert minutes per week to hours per week.
Minutes per week / 60 minutes per hour = Hours per week
180 minutes / 60 minutes per hour = 3 hours per week

Step 3: Calculate the total hours of television watched in 2 weeks.
Hours per week × 2 weeks = Total hours in 2 weeks
3 hours per week × 2 weeks = 6 hours

Therefore, the children 6-11 years old watch 6 hours of television in 2 weeks.
