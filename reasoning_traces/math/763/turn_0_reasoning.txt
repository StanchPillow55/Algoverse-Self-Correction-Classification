- Each gift will use 2 yards of ribbon.

Total ribbon needed = 6 × 2 yards = 12 yards

Step 2: Calculate the amount of ribbon left over.
Ribbon left over = Total ribbon - Total ribbon needed
Ribbon left over = 18 yards - 12 yards = 6 yards

Therefore, the amount of ribbon left over is 6 yards.
