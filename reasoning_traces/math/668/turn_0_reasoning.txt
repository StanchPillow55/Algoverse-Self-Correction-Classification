To solve this problem, we need to find the volume of the box and the volume of each building block, and then calculate how many building blocks can fit into the box.

Given information:
- The box is 8 inches in height, 10 inches in width, and 12 inches in length.
- The wooden building block is 3 inches in height, 2 inches in width, and 4 inches in length.

Step 1: Calculate the volume of the box.
Volume of the box = Length × Width × Height
Volume of the box = 12 inches × 10 inches × 8 inches = 960 cubic inches

Step 2: Calculate the volume of each building block.
Volume of the building block = Length × Width × Height
Volume of the building block = 4 inches × 2 inches × 3 inches = 24 cubic inches

Step 3: Calculate the number of building blocks that can fit into the box.
Number of building blocks = Volume of the box / Volume of each building block
Number of building blocks = 960 cubic inches / 24 cubic inches = 40 building blocks
