Given information:
- There are 10 children in the classroom.
- Each child has 7 books.
- The teacher brings another 8 books to the classroom.

Step 1: Calculate the total number of books the children have.
Number of children × Number of books per child = Total books the children have
10 × 7 = 70 books

Step 2: Calculate the total number of books in the classroom.
Total books the children have + Books brought by the teacher = Total books in the classroom
70 books + 8 books = 78 books

Therefore, the total number of books in the classroom is 78.
