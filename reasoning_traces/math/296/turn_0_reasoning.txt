Given information:
- Chelsea has 24 kilos of sugar.
- She divides the 24 kilos into 4 bags equally.
- One of the bags gets torn and half of the sugar in that bag falls to the ground.

Step 1: Divide the 24 kilos of sugar into 4 equal bags.
24 kilos ÷ 4 bags = 6 kilos per bag

Step 2: Determine how much sugar is in the bag that gets torn.
Since the bags are equal, each bag contains 6 kilos of sugar.

Step 3: Determine how much sugar is lost when half of the sugar in the torn bag falls to the ground.
Half of 6 kilos = 3 kilos of sugar lost

Step 4: Determine how much sugar remains.
Total sugar - Sugar lost = Remaining sugar
24 kilos - 3 kilos = 21 kilos

Therefore, the amount of sugar that remains is 21 kilos.
