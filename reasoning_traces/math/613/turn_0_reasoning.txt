Okay, let's work through this step-by-step:

Given information:
- John needs to replace both of his hearing aids.
- Each hearing aid costs $2500.
- Insurance covers 80% of the cost.

Step 1: Calculate the total cost of replacing both hearing aids.
Total cost = Cost of 1 hearing aid × 2
Total cost = $2500 × 2 = $5000

Step 2: Calculate the amount covered by insurance.
Amount covered by insurance = 80% of the total cost
Amount covered by insurance = 0.80 × $5000 = $4000

Step 3: Calculate the amount John has to pay out-of-pocket.
Amount John has to pay = Total cost - Amount covered by insurance
Amount John has to pay = $5000 - $4000 = $1000

Therefore, John has to pay $1000 out-of-pocket to replace both of his hearing aids.
